---
title: I am 25
author: Gregory Corso
type: verso
---

With a love a madness for Shelly
Chatterton<span style="display:inline-block; width:1em;"></span>Rimbaud
and the needy-yap of my youth
<span style="display:inline-block; width:3.5em;"></span>has gone from ear to ear:
<span style="display:inline-block; width:2em;"></span>I HATE OLD POETMEN!
Especially old poetmen who retract
who consult other old poetmen
who speak their youth in whispers,
saying:—I did those then
<span style="display:inline-block; width:3.7em;"></span>but that was then
<span style="display:inline-block; width:3.7em;"></span>that was then—
O I would quiet old men
say to them:—I am your friend
<span style="display:inline-block; width:5.75em;"></span>what you once were, thru me
<span style="display:inline-block; width:5.75em;"></span>you'll be again—
Then at night in the confidence of their homes
rip out their apology-tongues
<span style="display:inline-block; width:4.5em;"></span>and steal their poems.
